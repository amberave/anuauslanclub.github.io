# Cross Institutional Study Process

Guidance for students on the process to complete an Auslan course through La Trobe University and have it added to their ANU academic record as an elective.

### Links
* [ANU - Cross Institutional Study](https://www.anu.edu.au/students/program-administration/program-management/cross-institutional-study)
* [La Trobe University - Cross Institutional Study](https://latrobe.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/254/kw/cross%20insitutional)

**Warning: Forms and applications can take time to be processed. Please make sure the student has enough time to be approved.**

## Step 1: Applying through your (ANU) college 

**Warning: It is advised that the person applies in advance (semester, minimum 2 weeks before the course commences), or there could be a late application fee. This could vary between colleges.**

Complete the Application to Study Elsewhere on a Cross-Institutional Basis form. You need to email the college office with this form, alongside the course outline in a separate document.

Once approved, the college may have you complete another form to add a shell course. They will send it through with instructions. This doesn't have to be paid for, and it does not count towards your degree, it is just sitting there until they can add the course credit afterwards.

## Step 2: Apply to the host university

### Method 1: Applying via Open Universities Australia

Applying through Open Universities Australia before the application deadline. This is the method I used. 

Call them to help you apply for a form, so the class can be put on HECS. Then you are enrolled and will get an email to set up your account.

*Note: I recommend going through method 2*

### Method 2: Applying directly on the website.

**Warning: make sure the course applied for is the online session.**

Applications close before the start of the course. This may vary between universities. La Trobe Inbound application is due one week before.

You will need to have the following documents ready to submit with your application:

* home institution approval - letter given from your college
* USI
* Course code
* passport/drivers licence
* copy of your Visa (if applicable)
* attach a [certified copy](https://latrobe-current.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1585) of transcripts or evidence of completion of prior studies, with English translation (if applicable)
* current funding evidence from your home institution such as a statement of account

**Use your university email (not personal) when applying.** They will then send a code you need to put in before the application can start.

Access information and listings of La Trobe Auslan subjects: [La Trobe University Handbook](https://handbook.latrobe.edu.au/search?q=australian+sign+language) *(filter for 'Subjects')*

*Note: This is not the method I used, so there may be more things needed.*

## Step 3: 

**Warning: Make sure the student knows that if this course is their last course before graduating, other universities can release grades the same day, as the date for conferral approval. Hence they will have to wait to apply for out-of-session conferral.**

Once the course is completed apply to get the transcript. Once received, apply for course credit. Complete the Application for Course Credit form. Then email your College office with the Application, transcript, and the same course outline used in step 1. **Also good to mention that the cross-institutional study was approved in the email.**

When approved they will ask you to check academic history in ANUHub, I recommend checking your academic transcript too.
